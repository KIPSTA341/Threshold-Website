The gap

A mandate is not a venture portfolio.

No team to source, select and support the ventures. Building that team in-house means hiring, setup and a permanent cost.

The consequence: capital waits, or support runs without clear proof.

Start with the mandate

Select for outside demand and a result worth funding.

Who fits

A working offer, early traction or outside support, and a founder ready to work towards a clear next result.

Who doesn’t

Idea-only projects, ventures already sustainable, or no credible way to check progress.

  • Agree the missionMission, target group and public benefit.
  • Select for potential to progressFounder commitment, outside demand and a useful next result.
  • One next result eachA customer commits. Income repeats. An institution renews. One venture needs a first buyer; another needs repeat demand or lasting institutional support.

Different ventures. Different work.

The evidence standard stays consistent. The support changes with the bottleneck: focused sprints, coaching, AI and specialists.

Partner questions

BUILD is new. The first cohort will test the model.

  • Your roleAgree the mission and criteria. Confirm the venture pool at Month 3. Disburse capital on panel decisions.
  • Who verifies evidence?Threshold checks dated, attributable evidence. The independent panel reviews capital requests and decides.
  • What if progress stalls?Not an automatic exit. Review the bottleneck. Extend, adapt or pause. Further funding follows evidence.
  • Follow-upRecord the starting point at entry. Check outside evidence at Month 12. Then 3, 6 and 12 months later: did the result hold, what happened next, and who backed it?
