One venture

A venture with customers and a working offer, but uneven recurring revenue.

The constraint
Demand exists, but late handoffs and founder-only decisions cost repeat business
Baseline
Repeat revenue, overdue work, cancellations, gross margin and founder interventions
One two-week sprint
Assign an owner, document the failing handoff, test a simple operating rhythm; add supervised AI reminders only if useful
The check

Sponsor decision
Fund expansion, fix the system further or pause
The system

Not every venture needs the same route.

1 · BaselineMap the starting point

How the programming runs

The next sprint, not a fixed syllabus.

  • BaselineRecords one 90-day result, the current gate, the evidence route and the bottleneck.
  • The next sprintThe coach chooses the next two-week sprint from a 12-sprint menu. The 12 sprints are a menu, not 12 required classes: for example, find a buyer, shape a testable offer, move a live decision, repeat a first win, check the economics or hand off a founder-dependent process.
  • Use it within seven daysThe fellow builds an asset and uses it with a buyer, partner or funder within seven days.
  • Weekly pulseA weekly clinic or short voice or text check-in identifies the next move.
  • Days 30, 60 and 90Compare the evidence against the baseline.
The AI progression

Skills, then workflows, then selective agents.

1 · In useSkills

A reusable method for discovery, offers and insight.

2 · Being testedWorkflows

Account research, outreach drafting, meeting follow-up and pipeline review. Three real runs precede a repeatable workflow.

3 · Selective experimentPersonal agents

Supervised preparation and coordination as fellows become ready. Shadowing and human review precede an agent’s wider use.

Works with the tools founders already use; people approve external actions.

Support starts where the result is stuck
No clear offer

Build a priced offer. Put it in front of a real buyer.

No customer pipeline

Find relevant prospects. Send tailored outreach. Work the replies.

Pilots never repeat

Fix scope, pricing or delivery. Ask for the next order.

AI handles the repeatable. Founders handle the consequential.

  • Go-to-marketMap and score buyers. Draft tailored outreach. Track replies and keep the pipeline current.
  • FundraisingFind and score funders. Draft applications. Track evidence, deadlines and follow-ups.
  • OperationsTurn meetings and inboxes into owners, tasks, reminders and risk flags.
  • Founding cohort statusHuman support is ready. AI workflows are tested in delivery. Founders approve consequential actions.
