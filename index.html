Follow-up

Progress doesn’t end at graduation.

Year 1

What changed?

Starting point, work shipped and outside proof. A recommendation: back further, adapt or pause.

Year 2

Who backed it next?

New contracts, repeat demand and follow-on funding. Did the progress last?

Year 3+

Who benefited?

The livelihood, environmental or community outcomes linked to the mission.

Each follow-up answers a funding question, not just a reporting requirement. We report contribution, not proven causation.
