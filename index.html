The decision

Capital is not a prize. Capital has a job.

Every dollar and every hour should buy down uncertainty. What must we learn, prove or put in place for this venture to take its next step? We work backwards from that decision, then focus support and capital on the smallest useful action that reduces the uncertainty: a buyer test, a delivery improvement, a new operating system or evidence for a future funder.
